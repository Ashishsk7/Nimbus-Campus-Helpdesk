# Nimbus University Hostel Rules (MOCK DOCUMENT)

> Mock content written for a classroom assignment. Not a real policy.

1. **Curfew:** Residents must be inside the hostel by 22:00. Late entry needs a warden's written permission.
2. **Visitors:** Visitors are allowed only in the common room between 16:00 and 19:00 with a signed visitor slip.
3. **Quiet hours:** 23:00 to 06:00. No loud music or group calls in rooms.
4. **Appliances:** Heaters, induction cooktops and extension boards are not allowed in rooms.
5. **Room care:** Report damage to the warden within 24 hours. Damage costs may be charged to the residents.
6. **Leave:** Overnight leave needs a parent/guardian-approved leave form submitted a day in advance.
7. **Mess timings:** Breakfast 07:30-09:00, Lunch 12:30-14:00, Dinner 19:30-21:00.
